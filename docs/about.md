# About Voice

Voice is a clean and intuitive audiobook player that explores new technologies, design ideas, and modern coding practices. Our goal is to
create an app that is both reliable and a joy to use.

Key characteristics of Voice include:

- **User Focus:** Prioritizing intuitive and straightforward usage.
- **Clean, not minimal:** Features are welcome when they make listening better for many listeners. Every screen stays calm: it shows
  what you need while listening, and the rest is one tap away. Good defaults come before new settings.
- **Reliability:** Regular updates and community feedback help to keep the app stable.
